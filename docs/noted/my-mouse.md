## What is bad about it?
The mouse isn't bad but for osu! and in general there are some things i would also like to change or leave out for the project. This mouse is literally advertised as an MMO/MOBA mouse, it *works* for osu! but "works" is relative. 

### Weight
At 114g (according to Corsair), this mouse is **not light** and wasn't intended to be. It would be nice to keep a mouse in the range of 50-70g (this is nowhere near a final number). A spreadsheet of top osu players (excclusively with keyboard and mouse) shows lots of variety. Everyone is different and top players definitely *do* use heavy mice. You don't hit a ceiling just because you use a heavier mouse but what difference does weight make to the playing surface and grip?

### Grip
This is likely a skill issue but an issue i face using this mouse is my palm drifting, making it harder/ more uncomfortable to aim, even causing my hand to hit the playing surface. What fixes this? What makes a good grip?
